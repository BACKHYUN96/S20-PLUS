## Automated fixtures

The synthetic PCM and FLAC fixtures are generated by `tools/generate_fixtures.py` from deterministic integer values using FFmpeg. They contain no recorded music or third-party sound recordings. Full-scale edge values are for automated comparisons, not listening tests.

## v0.2 native USB prototype / Android NDK runtime

The usbfs transport, packet/peak checks and SHA-256 implementation are PureBit source under the project's license. No libusb, Oboe or native libFLAC binary is bundled. Linux usbfs structures/ioctls are consumed through the Android NDK system headers.

The native build statically links the NDK r28 C++ runtime/compiler support. Corresponding upstream notices, including libc++/libc++abi Apache 2.0 with LLVM exceptions and applicable runtime/header notices, are retained in `third_party/NDK-NOTICE` and `third_party/NDK-NOTICE.toolchain`, and in the APK assets.
